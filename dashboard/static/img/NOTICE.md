# The cockpit's images

`qwen4-27b.webp`, the art of the Qwen4 27B card in the Now view, was made on the reference box on
2026-11-06 by this repository's image lane: Qwen-Image 2.1 (`Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1`, the base model, 40 steps,
seed 42, 1536x1024), through its edits endpoint, from Qwen's mark of `logos/qwen.svg` (Lobe Icons 1.102.0, MIT,
see `logos/NOTICE.md`) laid on a dark background. The prompt asked for its three shapes as thick pieces of clear
crystal glass with beveled edges, kept in their exact shapes and layout, refracting light into subtle rainbow
highlights, lit by an electric blue rim light, standing on a glossy black floor with blue reflections, on a deep
navy background with a soft blue glow from the left, with no text. No other image went into it. It was scaled to
1200x800 and saved as WebP at quality 86.

Qwen-Image 2.1 is published under the Qwen Research License, which grants the use of the model for non-commercial
purposes only (its section 2a) and is not explicit about who owns what the model makes. This image is part of this
free and open source project on those terms. The mark it shows belongs to Alibaba Cloud, and the cockpit shows it
only to say which model the card announces.
